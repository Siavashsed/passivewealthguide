## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How can I start investing with only $100?

You can start today, and the plan is simpler than most people expect: open an account, buy one low-cost index fund, and automate your deposits.

**1. Pick the account.** If you have earned income, a Roth IRA is a strong choice because your money grows tax-free and you can withdraw your contributions if you really need them. If you want flexibility, a regular brokerage account works too. Fidelity, Schwab and Vanguard all let you open one online in minutes with no minimum balance.

**2. Buy one broad index fund.** A total market or S&P 500 index fund spreads your $100 across hundreds or thousands of companies. Look for an expense ratio below 0.10%. Many brokers offer fractional shares or zero-minimum funds, so you don't need to afford a whole share.

**3. Automate it.** Set up a recurring deposit, even $25 or $50 a month. This does two things: it builds the habit, and it removes the temptation to time the market.

**4. Leave it alone.** The biggest mistake beginners make is checking daily, panicking during a dip, and selling. Index funds reward patience.

**What to avoid:** individual stock tips, paying advisory fees on a tiny balance, and waiting until you have more money. The gap between investing and not investing matters far more than the exact fund you choose at the start.
